# Terminology: the Japanese words of the docs

This page is about the translations themselves, so it shows English and Japanese side by side. The other pages of the docs are in one language each. It holds the Japanese words that the pages of the docs rest on: the words of the rule books that the Japanese text of the drawings uses, and the Japanese names of the things the pages name in English. Every word of the drawings and the demo is in [strings-ja.md](./strings-ja.md); the Japanese names of parks, grounds and arenas are in [ballparks.ja.md](./ballparks.ja.md), [grounds.ja.md](./grounds.ja.md) and the atlas.

## Swimming, water polo, diving and artistic swimming

The Japanese text of the pools uses the words of the Japan Swimming Federation (日本水泳連盟) in its own rule books, read from swim.or.jp on 2026-10-13, and not a translation of World Aquatics' English. The main label is the rule's word; the everyday word that television and the newspapers use more is an alias, and is not written by the package.

## Skating

Where the Japan Skating Federation's own rule books are not on the web, the Japanese is the Japanese translation of the ISU's Special Regulations and Technical Rules Speed Skating (the 2018 edition, kept by the Aomori skating association), the Federation's teaching text (スピードスケート指導教本) and its viewing guide. Television's word, where it differs, is an alias.

| Part | The word | Where | Television's word (alias) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| The curves | カーブ | the same rules; the guide: カーブの半径8m | コーナー |

| The padding, the coaches' area, the pre-start line | 防護マット, コーチエリア, プレ-スタートライン | Rules 228.2, 227.3 and 227.2 | |
