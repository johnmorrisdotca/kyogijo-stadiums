## [0.41.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- **The crowd outside the venue** ([the page](./docs/outside.md)): `outside: { level: 0 | 25 | 50 | 75 | 100 }` on a [district](./docs/district.md) puts that share of the venues' seats' worth of people on the sidewalks, plazas, parks, platforms and grounds round them, in 2D (`mountDistrict`, new) and in the 3D turn (`mountDistrictTurn`, whose view has `setOutside(level)` and `outsideCost()`), never on a carriageway, a railway, water or a building. The crowd is one ordered list, so a level is a prefix of it (nobody moves when the level does), each venue's share is exact to a person at every level and the same options give the same list (`outsideCrowd`, `outsideCount`). It is drawn with no element for a person: two typed arrays, one loop and one `putImageData` on one canvas, laid over the plan or on the 3D floor, where the floor's own matrix carries it round; a district seen whole shows density and a near view shows each person, and nothing is painted again when the view turns. Measured at 100 percent for BC Place and Rogers Arena together (73,371 people): a paint of 2 to 17 ms on a level change (the first about twice that), 0.3 ms more of the main thread a frame while the view turns, 60 frames a second with it as without, and 250 ms once for the mask and the crowd.
- **The walkable mask of Vancouver** (BC Place and Rogers Arena, one mask shared): made from OpenStreetMap's own answer (Overpass, ODbL 1.0, kept raw in `data-sources/walk/2026-10-22/`, credited "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors") by `pnpm walk:fetch` and `pnpm walk` (`scripts/walk-rules.mjs` says what a person may stand on and what stops a crowd) into `src/walk.data.ts`, apart from the CC0 data. `src/outside.raw.test.js` holds all 73,371 people to the raw answer. Only Vancouver has a mask; every other venue comes in a later release, and so do the entrances and the walk in and out.
- **A bar of the five levels** on the demo's [district page](https://johnmorrisdotca.github.io/kyogijo-stadiums/district.html?outside=50), over the plan and the 3D view, kept in the address (`outside=50`), in English and Japanese, with the number of people said in words.
- `pnpm outside:cost` prints the cost of a crowd; `DistrictPlan.origin` is the point the plan's metres are measured from.

## [0.40.2] - 2026-10-21

### Fixed

- **BC Place and Lumen Field still differed between football and soccer, by a few pixels**: 0.40.1 made the frame the building's, but the view's origin still moved with the field, so the picture shifted and its margin changed, and the crowd's tiles started at the field's corner, so the people were a different pattern in each. A stadium of more than one configuration is now laid out in **the venue's own frame**, the middle of its field at the origin: the `viewBox` is the same text for every configuration, the building, the bowl and the crowd are the same numbers and the same tiles (`crowdStands(...).shift` is where the field's middle is in the surface's own frame, and the surface group is shifted by it), and the 3D view's floor, stands and what stands on the field are in the same frame (`uprightsFor` moves the field's things into it). Rendered at one size in a browser, the two configurations differ in no pixel outside the field's surround and the closed seats, in 2D and from overhead in 3D (`e2e/venue.demo.mjs`, Lumen Field too, light and dark, in Chromium and WebKit on macOS and Linux). In the dark the crowd's dimming (`opacity` on `.ky-crowd`) is kept on a layer of its own for a stadium of more than one configuration (`will-change: opacity`): without it Chromium on Linux rounded the dimmed crowd a level or two differently depending on what was drawn over it.
- **The seats a configuration does not open are closed in the same place every time**: BC Place's soccer pitch opens 52,497 of the bowl's 54,500 and Lumen Field's 37,722 of 68,740. A whole bowl that does not open is drawn as empty seats under a pale veil (`ring.closed`), and the seats left over are a block of the next bowl at the middle of the left side, as wide as comes nearest (`bowl.closedSectors`, a `closed` sector in the stands, painted in 2D and on the raked planes in 3D). It replaces the thinner crowd that the upper bowl had in 0.40.1; no random number is used.

## [0.40.1] - 2026-10-21

### Fixed

- **Switching a stadium between football and soccer reshaped the whole drawing** (BC Place, Lumen Field): the view was fitted to the field and the bowl was rebuilt from the configuration's seats, so the building's outline, the scale, the frame and the tint of the ground all changed. Now only the field changes. The view is the building's with a little ground round it, about the middle of the field (the same outline and scale for every configuration, in 2D and in 3D), the bowl is the building's and holds the most seats any configuration has (BC Place's soccer 52,497 opens from the lowest bowl up and draws the other 2,003 seats empty, as Lumen Field's does), and the ground round the building is the football field's beige whichever field lies in it (`data-stadium` on the drawing's root; the dark ground is one for every sport). `planStadium(...).total` for BC Place's soccer is now 54,500, the bowl's, with `open` the configuration's 52,497.
- **The stadiums page's buttons were the browser's own** (the venue buttons) and its field choice differed from the family's: the venues are chips and the field a segmented choice, as on the venues and home pages, 48 pixels high, with a pressed state, in light and dark, and on a phone the chips scroll sideways. A new demo test (`e2e/controls.demo.mjs`) fails on any visible button or select of any demo page that is left in the browser's own style, in light and dark.

## [0.40.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- **The named lighting presets** ([the presets](./docs/venues.md#the-lighting-presets)): `lighting: "game" | "intro" | "concert"` join `"night"` and `"lightsDown"`. `game` is white light with the house half lit (a light veil, `u-veil-half`) and a pool of white light round the surface, `intro` the house dark with the home team's colour washed over the surface and the visitors' (the second colour) in the aisle beams, `concert` the house dark with warm amber beams and a purple surface. The presets are a table of constants, `LIGHT_PRESETS` (each preset's `house`, `aisles`, `pool`, and wash and beam colours), with `LIGHTING_PRESETS` (the names) and `LIGHT_COLORS` (the named colours); `LIGHT_PRESETS`, `LIGHTING_PRESETS`, `LIGHT_COLORS` and `resolveLighting` are exported from the main entry and from `/turn` (the 0.39.0 notes named `LIGHT_COLORS` without exporting it), and `WASH` from `/turn`. The `intro`'s team colours are the package's where it has them: the crowd's `home` and `away`, else the colours of a score's two teams, else blue and amber. `lighting: { preset, colors }` passes colours (the first stands for the home team's, the second for the visitors'), and `wash` and `aisles` win over them, for any preset. `resolveLighting(option, team)` now answers `{ preset, house, aisles, pool, beam, wash }`. `TurnModel.lighting` and the view's `data-lighting` say the preset (the stylesheet has a rule for `game` and one for the three dark ones); in the plan the stands' group has `ky-half` or `ky-down`. The `celebration` (colours chasing round the bowl) is to come. Off by default, and a drawing without lighting is exactly what it was.
- **A Lighting choice on the demo**, in place of the Night and Lights down buttons (kept in the address as `lighting=intro` and so on, with `wash=` for the colour of light over the preset's own; an old address's `night=1` and `lights=1` still work): a choice in the bar over the venues page's view and the home page's featured arena, and on the stadiums, tournament layouts and district pages. The intro is shown with sample team colours (a red and a blue that are nobody's). In 3D a change goes in while the view turns, without stopping it or sending it back.

### Fixed

- **A test that raced another** (`src/docs-language.test.js`): it walks the Markdown files from the root, and met the docs examples' scratch folder `.cache` while that test was removing it, so that `pnpm check` failed now and then with an error reading a file that had just gone. `.cache` is skipped.
- **The demo's selects were 24 pixels high on a phone in WebKit** (`e2e/looks.demo.mjs`, "every control is a finger wide", failing on `webkit-phone` since 0.39.0): the bar's colour choice took the chip class, whose `display: inline-flex` WebKit does not honour on a select. The bar's selects are `display: inline-block`, 44 pixels high, no wider than 12 rem (they had taken the venue choice's 16 rem minimum).

## [0.39.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- **Lights down** (`lighting: "lightsDown"`, [lights down](./docs/venues.md#lights-down)): a show's house on the same `lighting` option as the night. The house is nearly black; each stair aisle is lit from above as a beam, drawn in the plane of the seats (so it follows the tier's rake) as a wide faint stroke, a narrower one and a bright core, which is the soft falloff on the steps either side, with two thin lit rails beside it (a vomitory has a broader beam) and the front of every step edged with a thin line of light; the boards glow as at night. In the plan the same, as `ky-cr-beam` and `ky-cr-edge` under a heavier veil. 3D, plan, a district, a tournament hall and the usual stands all take it.
- **A wash of colour of light on the surface** (`lighting: { preset, wash }`): one flat plane over the playing surface in 3D (`light-wash`, with a rink's rounded corners) and a rectangle in the plan (`ky-cr-wash`), a radial gradient brighter over the middle (`WASH`: 0.46 to 0.26, never so much that the markings under it are lost, and a test holds it), with the night's pool of light taking the colour as well. `aisles` is the colour of the beams. Both take a CSS colour (anything else is ignored) or a name from `LIGHT_COLORS`: `white` (the beams' default), `amber`, `purple`, `magenta`, `blue`, `cyan`. `resolveLighting` now answers `{ preset, aisles, wash }`; `LightingPreset` is `"night" | "lightsDown"` and `LightingOptions` takes `{ preset, aisles, wash }`. `TurnModel.lighting` and the view's `data-lighting` say the preset (`"lightsDown"` is darker than `"night"` in the stylesheet). Off by default, and a drawing without lighting is exactly what it was; the presets, the chase and the team colours of the show-lighting plan are to come, on the same option. The beams, edges and wash are static shapes (colours baked in): nothing is worked out as the view turns.
- **A Lights down button and a wash choice on the demo** (kept in the address as `lights=1` and `wash=purple`, `magenta`, `blue` or `white`): in the bar over the venues page's view and the home page's featured arena (Lights down and Night turn each other off; the beams take a colour that goes with the wash), and as a checkbox and a choice on the stadiums, tournament layouts and district pages. In 3D they change the lighting while the view turns, without stopping it or sending it back.

## [0.38.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- **Night lighting** (`lighting: "night"`, [night lighting](./docs/venues.md#night-lighting), `src/venue.lighting.ts`): a venue lit as at night. The house goes down (a veil over the seats, the venue's colours set dark in the light theme and in the dark one), the playing surface stays lit with a pool of light round it that is clear over the markings, small lights show along the aisles and stairs, the exits (every fourth aisle, the vomitories) are green, the ribbon boards and the scoreboard of [the LED boards](./docs/venues.md#led-boards) glow with a halo under every panel, and the windows, the coping and the lanes of the concourse are softly lit. `venue: { lighting }` in the 3D view, `crowd: { lighting }` in the plan (and read by the 3D view where `venue.lighting` says nothing), `stadium: { lighting }` round a tournament hall; `"night"`, `true` and `{ preset: "night" }` are the same and `false` is none. It is the base later lightings are added to: the option is a preset name, and `TurnModel.lighting` and the view's `data-lighting` say which. Nothing is worked out again as the view turns: the lights are custom properties (`--ky-night-aisle`, `--ky-night-exit`, `--ky-night-rim`) and a few static shapes, one added plane (`night-pool`) to a venue; `turn.update(options, { venue: { lighting } })` changes it while the view turns. The lights are at least seven to one against the seats in both themes (a test holds it). New exports: `LightingOptions` and `LightingPreset`. About 3 KB of source in the one new module.

### Changed

## [0.37.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- **LED boards** (`boards`, [the LED boards](./docs/venues.md#led-boards), `src/venue.boards.ts`): the ribbon boards along the fascia between the seating levels, and a scoreboard hung over the middle of the playing surface, for every real venue (the thirty-two arenas of the NHL, BC Place, Lumen Field, Korakuen Hall and the ballparks), for the usual stands, in a district, and, as `stadium: { boards }`, round a tournament hall. `venue: { boards: true | { ribbon, scoreboard, colors, pattern, score } }` in the 3D view, `crowd: { boards }` in the plan (and read by the 3D view where `venue.boards` says nothing); off by default, and a drawing without it is exactly what it was. The content is generic and ours: panels of colour in `bands`, `blocks`, `chevrons` or `waves`, and a plain score in seven-segment digits (paths, no font); the colours are the asker's, else the crowd's `home` and `away`, else amber, blue and white (any other string than a hex, `rgb()`, `hsl()` or a name is ignored). In 3D a ribbon is the top 3.8 feet of the suite level's wall, a plane of its own (the walk below it is the walk less that strip; a venue with one level has its ribbon on the front of that level), and the scoreboard is four faces with the score on each, a ring of display under them, a cap and a strut, hung over the surface by a fifth of its longer side and sized by it (an arena's is about 28 by 19 feet). A ballpark has no scoreboard over the field, a hall has the layout's own (only ribbons there), and a score drawing's own scoreboard replaces the venue's. Every lit shape carries the class `u-led` (`ky-cr-led` in the plan) over a dark housing, for a lighting layer to make glow; the planes are the kinds `ribbon` and `hung-board` (uprights `ribbon-boards` and `hung-board`, said in words in English and Japanese). `turn.update(options, { venue: { boards } })` changes them while the view turns. New exports: `LedBoardOptions` and `LedBoardPattern`. About 17 KB of source in the one new module (`dist/venue.boards.js`), a ribbon of a 32-arena venue being a few dozen planes.
- **An LED boards switch on the demo** (kept in the address as `boards=1`): a button in the bar over the venues page's view and the home page's featured arena, which draws it in the plan and in 3D and, in 3D, changes it without stopping or sending back the turn; a checkbox on the stadiums, tournament layouts and district pages.

## [0.36.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- **A diamond mowing pattern** (`stripePattern: "diamonds"`, `菱形` in the demo): the crosshatch of a ballpark's outfield, as in a photograph of T-Mobile Park. On a ballpark it is squares of 30 feet with their sides along the foul lines, from home plate out, lighter and darker alternately (two passes of 45-degree stripes crossing); on a football field and a soccer pitch it is squares of a band turned 45 degrees, one of them centred on the turf so that both ends are mown alike, whatever the size. It is cut to the fair ground or to the turf by a clip path. The infield grass stays one tone, and a separate infield pattern is not offered: the pattern is the outfield's. [Theming](./docs/theming.md), the gallery's mowing sections.
- **The demo's Mowing choice is for every grass sport**: a football field and a ballpark have it too (it was the soccer pitch's alone), with Diamonds among the choices; the soccer section of the home page shows the diamonds as one more card.

- **Breaking: the park status `"historic"` is now `"past"`.** `Park["status"]` is `"current" | "secondary" | "past"`, as for the grounds and the atlas; the six NPB parks that read `"historic"` read `"past"` (the type and the value in `PARKS`). Code that compares `park.status === "historic"` must compare with `"past"`.
- **Breaking: `Sport` and `SPORTS` grow from 6 to 20 sports.** The type was `"nfl" | "cfl" | "ifaf" | "nhl" | "nba" | "mlb"`; it now also has `"soccer"`, `"curling"`, the net sports (`"tennis"`, `"volleyball"`, `"badminton"`, `"pickleball"`, `"tabletennis"`) and the field sports (`"lacrosse"`, `"rugby"`, `"afl"`, `"gaelic"`, `"cricket"`, `"fieldhockey"`, `"handball"`). - **Breaking: the NHL trapezoid is 22 feet wide at the goal line, not 18.** `NHL.trapezoid.atGoalLine` was 18 feet; Rule 1.8 has 22 feet, the figure since 2014-15, when the trapezoid was widened by 2 feet each side (18 feet from 2005-06 to 2013-14, which `rulesFor("nhl", year)` and a drawing with `year` still give). The rink is drawn with the wider trapezoid by default.
- **Breaking: the NHL's hash marks are 5 feet 7 inches apart, not 5 feet 11 inches.** The marks beside an end-zone circle were drawn 5 feet 11 inches apart, which added the lines' width to a gap it does not enter; Rule 1.9 says 5 feet 7 inches (`NHL.hashApart`). The AHL, ECHL, OHL, WHL and PWHL, which share the NHL's Rule 1, change with it. docs/sources.md also records where the NHL's Rule 1.8 (a 22-foot trapezoid at the goal line, drawn) and Rule 27.8 (18 feet) disagree.
- **Breaking: MLB bases are 18 inches square, not 15.** `MLB.baseSize` was 15 inches; Rule 2.03 of the Official Baseball Rules has 18 inches from the 2023 season. The size is now a marking in `rulesFor("mlb", year)` (`baseSize`, 15 inches before 2023, 18 from 2023, with `eraChanges("mlb")` listing it), so the field is drawn with 18-inch bases by default and 15-inch ones for a season before 2023.
- **Breaking: the SVG written by `drawKyogijo` changes.** Every NHL drawing (the trapezoid and the hash marks) and every MLB drawing (the bases) differs from 0.1.0's, so a stored copy of an old drawing, or a snapshot test of one, needs to be made again. Every drawing's stylesheet is also about 2 kB larger.

### Fixed

- **NHL hash marks were 3 feet apart before 2014-15.** The rulebook of 2012-13 (Rule 1.9) says "three feet (3') apart", and the NHL and NHLPA release of 6 October 2014 moves them to 5 feet 7 inches for 2014-15. `rulesFor("nhl", year).rules.hashApart` is 3 feet through 2013-14 and 5 feet 7 inches after, `eraChanges("nhl")` lists both (`nhl.hashApart.2012`, `nhl.hashApart.2014`) and a rink drawn for an earlier season has the marks 3 feet apart. Seasons before 2012-13 are cited by no rulebook read, so they keep 3 feet and `gaps` names `hashApart`. The AHL, ECHL, OHL, WHL and PWHL are unchanged: no page read dates a change of their marks.
- **docs/sources.md cited the wrong baseball rule for home plate.** Home plate is Official Baseball Rules 2.02 (Home Base) in the 2024 edition; 2.03 is The Bases. The 90-foot square is 2.01, not 2.01 and 2.02.

- **Theming.** Every colour is a custom property in a light and a dark set, `colors` sets them from code, and a court has its own look (`court`) with five to start from in `COURT_THEMES`.
- **Four ways in.** The function, `mountKyogijo` for a container, the `<kyogijo-field>` custom element with parts, and a frame (`embed.html`) for a page that runs no script; `@johnmorrisdotca/kyogijo/react` for React, which does not import it.
- **Accessible.** Each drawing is an `img` with a title and a description in English or Japanese.
- **The demo** with every sport and every park, wide and tall, light and dark, badges, compact cards, court looks, a live score, snippets for the four ways in, and the API reference.
